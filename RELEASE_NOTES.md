## DoomClipper 2026.11.25 — first Windows release

Turn long GTA 6 streams and videos into captioned vertical clips, on your own computer.

**Download** `DoomClipper-Setup-2026.11.25.exe` below (about 145 MB), run it, and let it finish its downloads.
See the [README](https://github.com/imperfec-t/Doomclipper-Release#install) for the full guide.

### In this version
- Clips from YouTube, Twitch, Kick, X, Reddit, Streamable, Medal links or your own files
- Face + game layouts with automatic webcam detection, picture-in-picture, and the DoomClipper frame
- Word-by-word captions in several styles, fixable word by word in Studio; headings with emoji; end cards
- "Top 5" countdowns with an AI voice-over and royalty-free music
- Live recording with automatic clips, Mark start / End mark, and Best moment of the last 3 minutes
- AI: sign in with your Claude or ChatGPT plan, or use an API key; a backup takes over when a plan hits its limit
- Posting to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram; alerts on Discord or Telegram

### Install notes
- Installs for your Windows user only (no administrator prompt). Needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- At the end it downloads the English speech model and the AI voice (about 830 MB, once).
- Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info →
  Run anyway**. You can check the download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`:
  `Get-FileHash DoomClipper-Setup-2026.11.25.exe -Algorithm SHA256`
